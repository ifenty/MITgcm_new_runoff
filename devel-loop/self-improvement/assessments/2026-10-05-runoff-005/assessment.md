# Assessment: doc_contract stale truncates its hits list (RUNOFF-005, 2026-10-05)

- **What happened:** correction round 2 recorded, in `devel-loop/documentation_contract.md`, that a figures row of `12` "matched 199 unrelated lines in this repository". Review A measured 335 over the inventory (191 excluding `MITgcm/`) and could not reproduce 199 under any scoping. The implementer then reproduced it exactly: `stale_lines` returns `{'lines': len(hits), 'hits': hits[:limit], 'truncated': …}` with `limit=200`. With the default cap the call reported `lines` 336, returned 200 hits, truncated 136 — and exactly **199** of the *returned* hits carried the figure. 199 is a count of the truncated list, not of the matches.
- **The defect:** the truncation is reported honestly in `truncated`, but the returned `hits` list is the natural thing to count, and nothing about it says it is partial at the point of use. An agent or script that counts entries of `hits` instead of reading `lines` understates a sweep, silently, by as much as the cap allows.
- **Why it mattered here rather than being harmless:** the understated figure went into *permanent prose in the documentation contract*, inside the paragraph that teaches agents not to trust a clean sweep. It survived one reviewer pass and was caught only because review A re-measured a number it had no particular reason to doubt. The figure also could not be guarded by the mechanism it described — a `199` figures row is exactly the bare-number row that paragraph tells you not to enrol.
- **Cost:** two reviewer re-measurements, one correction round for a single clause, and three agent turns of forensics — which established both the mechanism and the exact arithmetic: 199 is the capped list of 200 minus the one self-reference at documentation_contract.md:172. Three retellings of the totals were wrong before the right one, which is itself evidence for how easily this return shape is misread. Small in isolation; the concerning part is that the failure mode is silent and the artefact looks like a plausible measurement.
- **Classification:** kit usability defect, Medium. Not an integrity defect — `lines` and `truncated` are both reported, so the information is available — but the ergonomics invite the error, and the one place it landed was the contract that governs how figures are recorded.
- **Fix:** upstream in ESX-Team. Make the partial list self-describing, so a count of it cannot be mistaken for a total: see TEAM-DOCCONTRACT-STALE-HITS-TRUNCATION-001.
